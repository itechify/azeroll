# Goldshire survey data

Generated by `azeroll::survey`, enabled only by `WOW_AZEROLL_SURVEY=<directory>`.
Coordinates are WoW yards: +X north, +Y west, +Z up. `START=(-9450,60,57)`.

- `placements.json` / `placements.csv`: original ADT MDDF doodads and MODF buildings whose placement origins are within 600 yd horizontally of START, deduplicated by `(kind, uniqueId)`. Synthetic scatter and mode creatures are excluded. WMO-internal MODD furniture is not an ADT placement and is not enumerated here.
- `radius`: scaled M2 bounding sphere; WMO half-diagonal of the union of group AABBs. A null/empty radius means the asset did not resolve, never a zero-size object. These are visual bounds, not collision or pickup sizes.
- `rotation_deg`: original ADT Euler components. `yaw_deg` is the stored Y component, before the engine's placement basis conversion; use `placement_rotation` to reproduce orientation.
- WMO `world_aabb`: transform all corners of all group boxes, then union in WoW world coordinates. CSV `footprint_x`/`footprint_y` are the resulting axis-aligned dimensions. They include roof/overhangs and do not describe walkable space. WMO scale is 1 in this format.
- `terrain.csv`: 10-yard lattice over the 1200×1200 bounding square; missing rows mean missing terrain/hole/unloaded coverage, never inferred flat ground. Height is MCNK ground, not a building floor. Water comes from `WorldPoint::water_surface_at(Subject::Player, ground+0.1)` in a settled streamed neighbourhood; blank means no reported water surface. It is not a swimming-depth test.
- `inn-floor-probes.csv`: one-yard columns around Goldshire, downward body collision rays starting at z=62/67/72, capped at 30 yd. `hit_z` can be floor, furniture, roof or terrain; compare neighbouring columns and the different ray starts before choosing a floor.
- `reference-npc-anchors.csv`: separately sourced ClassicDB NPC coordinates, not read from client map files. Offline Azeroll does not load those NPC spawns.
- `shots.json`: nominal overhead bounds. `img/` contains locally captured game art and is gitignored. Overheads use a square orthographic projection and are resampled square after capture; obliques use the normal perspective projection.

Normal play registers none of this module's resources or systems. The survey holds Ready, teleports the ball/player focus through nine 400-yard squares, raises farclip to 550 yd, waits for presentable streaming, and then takes six oblique views. Dumps are checkpointed after each stop. A timed-out stop is logged and can leave incomplete coverage; inspect the run log and sample count.

## Supplemental archive checks

`inventory-validation.json` independently reads MDDF/MODF records in all 12 ADTs touching the 600-yd circle (tile columns 30–33, rows 48–50), using Python mpyq against patch-2, patch, wmo, terrain and base archives. It compares `(kind,uid)` against the runtime inventory. `inn-portals.json` transforms the inn root's MOPV/MOPT vertices into WoW world space and includes MOPR group references/flags. The transform for these untilted placements is yaw `(ADT rotation[1] - 180)` about WoW +Z. Portal 0 connects an exterior-flagged group to the entrance vestibule. Portal apertures are useful clearance bounds, not a substitute for a swept ball/trophy collision test.

`nearby-mine-anchors.json` separately reads adjacent ADT MODF records to locate mines outside the inventory radius, including Jasperlode (`md_spidermine`). These extra anchors are not added to the radius-limited placement manifest. `mine-exterior-portals.json` contains portals linked to a group flagged exterior; not every such portal is a surface entrance (some are horizontal/internal seams). All supplemental data are coordinates and paths, not rendered art. Temporary extraction scripts remain in `/tmp/az-read-map.py`, `/tmp/az-verify-inventory.py`, and `/tmp/az-mine-portals.py` for this session; they are not game code.
