# Azeroll progress and next steps

The short, current view of where the game stands and what to do next. Decisions and evidence live in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) (D-numbers below); design grammar in
[katamari-room-design.md](katamari-room-design.md). Update this file when a track lands.

## Direction (from the user)

- A genuine Katamari Damacy experience in Azeroth: the clump, the Prince, the camera, the size
  progression, and above all **levels with character and intention**. Dense themed vignettes, lines of
  items that pull you along, ramps to every surface that holds items, and no invisible walls inside the
  play space; contain the player with visible blockers. Size difficulty for a first-time human: the
  autopilot should win in ≤ ~40 % of the limit.
- A three-level campaign on one world: **Last Call at the Lion's Pride** (inn), **Market Day Mayhem**
  (Goldshire market), **Stormwind or Bust!** (Elwynn → Stormwind; eat the city, then the Cathedral).
- The hub is the planet Azeroth.
- Test builds go to the user's Windows PC by Taildrop.
